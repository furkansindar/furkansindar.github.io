About Me

Who Am I

I’m Furkan Sindar, an international, first-generation student from Turkey, now studying Enterprise AI at NJIT’s Ying Wu College of Computing as a Dorman Honors Scholar. I expect to finish my B.S. in May 2028 and continue into NJIT’s BS/MS program.

I transferred to NJIT from Madison College, where I earned dual associate degrees in Web Software Development and GenAI Front-End Development, and worked as an undergraduate research assistant.

My deep focus is human-AI interaction: how people and AI systems can work together well. My second lane is AI product management and AI-driven business model innovation.

Right now I serve as Product Manager in the Inborn GT Fellowship, where our team is building an AI-powered EV web app.

My honors project, Sip Success Online, was an accessible website for my own bubble tea business in the Philippines, built with input from the Wisconsin Council of the Blind. It won 1st place at the Fall 2024 Honors Competition and was presented at the 2025 Upper Midwest Honors Conference.

Outside of class, I founded a bubble tea business in the Philippines, led student government work in Wisconsin as a senator and governor, and wrote for my college newspaper.

My technical skills include HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Java, Python, SQL, Node.js, React, n8n, Figma, and AWS tools. I also hold the Google AI Professional Certificate.

My long-term goal is a PhD in human-AI interaction, after the BS/MS at NJIT, and a career building AI products that work for the people technology usually leaves behind.
