Product Manager, Inborn GT Fellowship

In the Inborn GT Fellowship, I work as Product Manager on a cross-functional fellow team building an AI-powered EV web app for a simulated NYC dealership's EV division.

The project runs a 14-week cycle from discovery to deployment: understanding the EV buyer, defining what the product should do, and shipping a working web app with AI features woven in.

My work centers on keeping the product focused: turning a broad EV concept into a clear scope, coordinating the team across Asana, Figma, Slack, and OneDrive, and making sure what we build serves a real user need rather than adding AI for its own sake.

This is where my AI product management direction gets practical: research sense from my NJIT work, applied to shipping a product with a team.
