Connected Care 2031

A five-year AI vision for a regional healthcare network

Kyndryl AI Studio · NJIT, November 2026

Student workshop challenge (Challenge 1, The Blank Canvas Workshop). I presented the vision in a 10-minute pitch to the studio.

The task

At NJIT's Kyndryl AI Studio (November 2026), the Blank Canvas Workshop asked for an executive vision of care in five years. The network's CIO said: "Surprise us."

The problem

Four pain points, one root cause. Referrals took 11 days on average and 22% were never completed. Clinicians spent 2+ hours a day on documentation. Patient data sat in 9 disconnected systems. Nursing turnover ran at 18% a year. The systems did not talk to each other, so people fell through the cracks.

The solution

One data layer connects all nine systems, with two AI capabilities on top. An AI referral coordinator checks every specialist's calendar and the patient's insurance, and offers 2 to 3 options before the patient leaves the clinic. An ambient documentation assistant drafts the note while the clinician reviews, corrects and signs. Clinicians make every final decision, privacy is built in, and everything starts with a pilot.

The targets

  • Referral wait: 11 days to 3 days
  • Incomplete referrals: 22% to under 5%
  • Documentation: 2+ hours to under 1 hour per day
  • Nursing turnover: 18% to about 10%

These are targets to validate in a pilot, not measured results.

My role

Solo research, design and presentation. 8-slide deck, 10-minute talk.
